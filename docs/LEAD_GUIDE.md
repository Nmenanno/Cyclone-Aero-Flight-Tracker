# Lead quick start

1. Sign in with your team-authorized email. Open **Teams**, then your team's page.
2. Start with **Next actionable task**. Read its Definition of Done, date and evidence requirements.
3. Use **Start task** or **Add update** to record progress. Blocked work stays visibly blocked until every predecessor is complete.
4. Use **Submit deliverable** for an attachment, HTTPS link or written report. Then **Mark complete**. Critical/reviewed work stays **Awaiting review** until a Director approves.
5. If the date is at risk, use **Request extension**. Explain progress, the delay and recovery plan. The active date stays unchanged until approval.
6. Before the next leads meeting, review your blockers and the meeting agenda. Bring decisions needed from other teams.

After a successful save, the next task and dashboard recompute. Other devices refresh automatically within 15 seconds. If you see an error, the change is not confirmed: retry or refresh as instructed. Do not treat a source-plan preview as live project status.
