# Team quick start

1. Open the tracker and pick your team.
2. Enter the shared team code and press **Unlock updates**. No email login is needed.
3. Read your next task and its **Task details**.
4. Press **Start task** while working, then **Mark done** when finished.
5. To undo completion, expand **Other tasks** and press **Reopen**.

If a task is waiting on earlier work, those tasks must be done first. Click their IDs to see them. Other devices refresh automatically within 15 seconds. A failed save is not a confirmed update; read the message and refresh if another person changed the task.

Refreshing the page or selecting **Lock updates** clears the shared code. Ask your team lead for it; do not post it publicly. Marking a task done records progress and does not constitute flight approval.
